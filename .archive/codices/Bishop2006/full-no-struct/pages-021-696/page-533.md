# 10.7.2 Expectation propagation on graphs

So far in our general discussion of EP, we have allowed the factors f i ( θ ) in the distribution p ( θ ) to be functions of all of the components of θ , and similarly for the approximating factors f ( θ ) in the approximating distribution q ( θ ) . We now consider situations in which the factors depend only on subsets of the variables. Such restrictions can be conveniently expressed using the framework of probabilistic graphical models, as discussed in Chapter 8. Here we use a factor graph representation because this encompasses both directed and undirected graphs.
