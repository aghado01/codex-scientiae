The stability of the MM -proposal under contamination settings C 1 to C 3 is appreciated in Figs. 3 to 6 and S.7 to S.18 as well as in Tables 4 and 5. In particular, the general structure of the additive functions has been better captured by the robust MM -fit which leads to narrower envelopes in the functional boxplots. In most situations, the mean integrated squared bias of the robust MM -estimates is quite similar to those obtained for clean samples. The only exception is when estimating η 2 under Model 1, where the increase on the bias observed under C 2 and C 3 may be explained by the outlying curve appearing in the functional boxplots (see Fig. S.12). In contrast to the described behavior of the least squares or quantile methods, our robust proposal provides more reliable estimates of the additive components under the considered contaminations which generally do not affect them.
