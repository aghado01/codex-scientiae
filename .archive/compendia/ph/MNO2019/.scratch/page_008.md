## 3.2 A Conjugate Family of Prior Intensities: Gaussian Mixtures

This section focuses on constructing a family of conjugate prior intensities, i.e., a collection of priors that yield posterior intensities of the same form when used in Equation (3). Exploiting Theorem 3.1 with Gaussian mixture prior intensities, we obtain Gaussian mixture posterior intensities. As PDs are stochastic point processes on the space W , not R 2 , we consider a restricted Gaussian density restricted to W .
