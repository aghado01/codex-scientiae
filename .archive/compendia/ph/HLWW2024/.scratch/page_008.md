We will now operate under the following.

Assumption. Given an oriented Poincaré fibration $ \pi : E \to B $, with typical fibre $ F $, then $ \pi $ is fibre homotopy equivalent to a block bundle with typical fibre a homology manifold (homotopy equivalent to $ F $) if and only if the just described section $ \operatorname{tso}(\pi) $ vanishes.

As indicated above, when the typical fibre $ F $ is a closed topological manifold, this follows from a family version of the results of [BFMW96], which Weinberger confirmed to us is valid. In particular, when $ \operatorname{tso}(\pi) $ vanishes and $ B $ is a manifold, $ E $ is homotopy equivalent to a homology manifold.

- (i) Giving a trivialisation of the fibrewise periodic total surgery obstruction $ \operatorname{tso}(\pi) $, and
- (ii) giving a lift of the fibrewise normal orientation $ \sigma_n(\pi) $ to a section of $ \Omega^d [ (E, B) \otimes_B L^s(\mathbb{Z}) ] $.
