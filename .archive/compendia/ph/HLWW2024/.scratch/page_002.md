- (iii) When P is an oriented Poincare´ complex of dimension d , there is a canonical point σ vs ( P ) in Ω d L vs ( P ), called the visible symmetric signature of P , see [Ran92, Example 9.13] and [CDH + 23, Corollary 4.4.20]. These data fit into the following commutative square whose left vertical arrow is the canonical forgetful map between oriented Poincare´ bordism and oriented normal bordism [Ran79].


$$
\begin{CD}
\Omega^{SP}(X) @>>> L^{vs}(X) \\
@VVV @VVV \\
\Omega^{SN}(X) @>>> X \otimes MSG
\end{CD}
$$

The upper horizontal map takes the (Poincare´ bordism class of a) map f : P → X to f ! ( σ vs ( P )). In particular σ vs ( P ) is the image of [id P ] in Ω SP n ( P ), see [Ran92, Section 19]. Oriented normal bordism satisfies excision giving an equivalence Ω SN ( X ) ≃ X ⊗ MSG, and the lower horizontal arrow for X = ∗ is called Ranicki’s normal orientation , while the top horizontal arrow is often referred to as the Sullivan–Ranicki orientation .
