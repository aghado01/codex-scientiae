### The predeclared decision rule (review amendment, adopted verbatim)

"Let measurements pick" was underdetermined: A3 and D optimize different objectives, and
measurements establish *feasibility*, never *valuation*. The valuation is fixed now, consistent
with the post-render objective (a missing captioned float is the loudest defect a reader sees):

1. A3-0 reveals surviving letters/words + a bounded rescue path → **A3 first**.
2. A3 requires global DLA work AND D-0 shows clean target/negative separation → **D first**.
3. A3 is engine-deep AND D lacks clean alignment or equation separation → **take F (or E1)**;
   force neither implementation.

Calibrate before implementing (probe first; expect the premise to move). Gate BOTH corpora on every
increment (baseline variant must reproduce recorded numbers before the knob variant means anything).
PRIMARY invariance non-negotiable — prefer structural (pipeline placement) over empirical. Read the
render; eyeball crops before default-on. Commit probes under `scratch/` (force-add past the ignore).
New thresholds only as relative, em-normalized statistics of proven species.

**New clauses (this re-mint):**
- **Probes must localize, not merely detect** — a binary existence probe collapses failure
  locations with different fixes; layer the probe along the lane pipeline (A3-0 form).

  measurements establish feasibility; valuation is a policy choice made in advance.

## §5 — Lessons register (0713's five stand; this re-mint adds three)

1–5. Unchanged from 0713 (premises are hypotheses; pipeline order beats geometry; clamp at contact
scale; PowerShell array/`$PID`/gitignore traps; selection semantics ≥ metrics).

6. **A binary probe collapses failure locations.** "Do the words exist?" cannot distinguish
   extraction, assembly, block, fragmentation, and attachment failures — and each has a different
   fix. Layer probes along the pipeline so the answer localizes.
7. **Aggregate acceptance invites cancellation.** A −10 that improves to −2 can hide missing
   diagrams offset by false equation crops — the same failure two-population scoring was built to
   kill at PRIMARY. Assertion work needs object-level truth.
8. **Cross-objective forks need predeclared valuation.** Feasibility is measurable; the worth of a
   missing captioned float vs a SECONDARY reduction is not — decide it before the probes run, or
   the probes decide it for you post-hoc.
