Committed as `7739228`. Now the direct answer to your question, because it has a crisp shape once you apply your constraint — "must work from this document's data alone, no run history" — as the filter:

**Random forest, classic form: no.** It's supervised like XGBoost; same conflict, just with bagging instead of boosting. Switching to it would change nothing philosophical.

**Random forest, Breiman's unsupervised trick: yes — this is the interesting survivor.** Train a forest per-document to discriminate real lines from a synthetic copy with permuted marginals (joint structure destroyed, marginals kept). No labels, no history. What you harvest is the **proximity matrix** — the fraction of trees where two lines co-land in a leaf — which is a per-document, data-adaptive similarity that handles mixed continuous/categorical _natively_. That's the principled fix for the one confessed hack in stage-0 (the arbitrary `FlagWeightPt = 2.0` scaling that makes a style flip "equal" a 2pt size step). And notice what a proximity matrix _is_: a coupling matrix — it could feed HDBSCAN or drop straight into the SPC engine. But stage-0 says the hand-chosen space isn't limiting yet, so this is filed as the upgrade path, not the plan.

**Isolation forest: yes, cleanly.** Unsupervised by construction, per-document, tiny, trivially dep-free. Its role is line-level anomaly _scoring_ below and beside the mode structure — a continuous work-list router for the long tail HDBSCAN's min-cluster-size can't see. Filed as an optional probe.

**But the tree you actually need most, you already built.** The condensed tree _is_ the arboreal method — stage-0's whole finding lives in it. And the completion role your GMM played gets a tree-shaped, fully transient analog: fit a shallow CART _to this document's own cluster labels_, use it to assign stragglers, keep its rules as the per-document audit record ("size ≥ 11.9 ∧ bold → mode 7"), discard it. Zero transfer, zero history, and it's _more_ legible than your GMM was — the completer's logic is readable per paper.

The piece XGBoost was carrying — role _semantics_ — moves to where I think it always belonged under your doctrine: **ordinal priors from universal typographic convention**, evaluated over cluster-relative mode statistics. "Body is the dominant mode; a heading is rarer, larger-or-bolder, precedes whitespace, and appears in legal sequence" is typography's physics — ordinal and relational, never an absolute pt value, so it passes the no-magic-strings test in a way a learned ensemble arguably never did. And the oracle gets _demoted, not discarded_: trainer → validator. The 66 papers become the offline bench that measures per-rule accuracy and — this is the part that matches your "then I may forgo" — quantifies exactly how often forgoing happens and where that residue routes to the membrane. Supervision as evaluation keeps the runtime unsupervised while still cashing the oracle asset.
