1. Roadmap premises are hypotheses: two falsified pre-implementation this arc (B's acceptance
   direction — inflow ROSE, split-feeds-veto; C's global band), one caught at the gate (placement).

   placement made C′'s PRIMARY safety structural).

   scale first.

   counting); `$PID` is reserved; scratch/ is gitignored — calib probes are force-added.

   distance — no metric fixes a selection defect.
