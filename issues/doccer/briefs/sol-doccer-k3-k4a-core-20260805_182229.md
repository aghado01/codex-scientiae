## 2. Located reference carrier

`LocatedRelation` is an immutable, canonically enumerated geometry value. Construction validates
the exact window and every edge against one representative master, sorts by `(Start, End)`, and
collapses duplicate extents. Equality and hash use `TextMaster.IsCompatibleWith` identity plus the
exact window and extensional edge set, matching the existing geometry-only `SpanSet` precedent.

The complete scalar-valid diagonal is materialized by `Identity`. `Seq` uses only endpoint equality
and therefore composes diagonal empties without borrowing Allen `Meets`. `Consuming` removes all
diagonal edges. `Reachability` performs direct finite graph traversal over consuming edges and adds
the complete declared-window diagonal; it is the one Boolean geometry closure K4a results will
delegate to. There is no compressed, incremental, provenance-bearing, or all-path backend.

`TextSlice.ToParent(LocatedRelation)` and `ToChild(LocatedRelation)` translate both the edges and
declared window. Child-to-parent is total. Parent-to-child is loud unless the whole relation window
lies inside the slice. Exact union, `Seq`, identity, and reachability commutation are executable.

## 3. Exact candidate graph and projection seam

`CandidateRegionGraph.Create` accepts one exact `ClaimSelection` and one exact window. The graph
retains that selection object; `Source` is its exact `SpanBatch` basis, and selected ordinals are the
graph edge identities. Every selected claim must be nonempty and wholly contained in the window.
Construction does not clip, filter, expand, or reorder the candidate population.

`ToLocatedRelation()` copies only selected claim spans into the compatible geometry carrier.
Parallel ordinals with equal spans collapse at that call. Graph equality remains reference-strict on
the source batch, while projections of different compatible equal-row batches may compare equal.
No reverse projection exists.

## 4. Assurance

The bounded located oracle uses the complete six-extents relation carrier on three boundaries:

- all 64 relation values are constructed;
- all 4,096 ordered compositions agree with an independently written nested-pair oracle;
- all 64 reachability values agree with independent Floyd-Warshall closure;
- all 262,144 relation triples satisfy associativity and both distributive laws through cached
  production composition results;
- consuming powers close within `boundary count - 1` and the next power is empty.

Additional cases cover Unicode scalar boundaries, the empty window, diagonal-empty adjacency,
compatible-master equality/hash, unequal-window and incompatible-master refusal, exact slice
round-trip and commutation, graph containment refusal, exact-batch identity, and parallel-edge
collapse. A test-only monotone collapsing-point counterexample retains the strict direct-image
inclusion reason without adding a generalized map API.

These finite reference contracts do not activate Lean. Reapply the existing burden gate before an
accelerated closure claims equivalence or a generalized map reopens exactness.
